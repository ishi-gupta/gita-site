A few quiet minutes with a very old book

Gandhi, Thoreau, Emerson and Oppenheimer all read the Gita. It begins with a man on a battlefield who doesn't know what to do, which is still a good place to start.

Breathe

A guided meditation of about seven minutes, with a story to picture, breath work and one verse to carry into your day.

Read

All 18 chapters as books you can flip through or listen to, in Sanskrit and in plain English or Hindi.

Talk

Call Gita or type. Tap a verse Gita mentions and read it for yourself.

Remember

A short daily quiz, a streak, and a map that fills in as you meet more of the book.

Good questions

Do I need to be Hindu, or know the Gita?

No. It's for anyone, whatever you believe, and you can start from zero. Talk in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Whose translation is it?

Our own. We made the English and Hindi from the public-domain Sanskrit and cross-checked them against two older public-domain translations, by Annie Besant and K. T. Telang.

When can I get it, and what will it cost?

iPhone comes first, then Android. It will be a paid subscription, with seven free days for new subscribers.

What do you keep when I sign up?

Your email, your phone type and reason for joining if you pick them, the site or link that brought you here, and the date. We use it to email you about the launch and the Kickstarter. Ask us to remove you any time, using the contact in our privacy policy.
